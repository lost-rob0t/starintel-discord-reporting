# STEWART'S WAS ASKED TO HOST FLOCK READERS AT FIVE RENSSELAER COUNTY STORES. IT SAID NO.

The Rensselaer County Sheriff's Office approached Stewart's Shops in August 2025 about hosting law-enforcement-monitored plate readers at five county stores.

By December, Stewart's had declined. Spokesperson Robin Cooper cited customer privacy concerns and questioned whether a private company should host the system instead of government property.

Hudson Police separately approached Stewart's about a Columbia County site. Stewart's declined that proposal too.

These were proposed host sites, not Stewart's Flock deployments. Do not count them as deployed cameras without separate evidence.
