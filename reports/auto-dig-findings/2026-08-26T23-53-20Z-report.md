# SYRACUSE SET A MAY 26 FLOCK REMOVAL DEADLINE. POLICE KEPT USING THE READERS FOR NEARLY ANOTHER MONTH.

Syracuse Common Council revoked Flock's city access on March 23. The city then set May 26 as the deadline for the readers to be removed.

Central Current later reported that Syracuse police kept using the Flock readers for nearly another month after that deadline.

By July, all 13 Flock readers on city property were gone. Syracuse moved to a separate Axon deployment: 26 readers under a five-year, $422,000 agreement.

The deadline existed. Operational use continued after it.
