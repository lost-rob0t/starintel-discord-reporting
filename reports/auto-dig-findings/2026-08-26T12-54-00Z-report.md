# RENSSELAER'S EIGHT FLOCK CAMERAS COST $48K. THE CONTRACT SAYS 30-DAY RETENTION.

The county paperwork fills in the part the earlier reporting did not have.

Rensselaer County Resolution G/112/25 authorized the Sheriff's Office to buy **eight Flock Falcon plate readers plus FlockOS for $48,000 over 24 months**, after a stated $10,000 discount. The order form lists a **30-day retention period** and says the purchase used a New York State Law Enforcement Technology grant.

The county also approved Flock as the sole-source vendor for the cameras, software, and cloud service, so the purchase did not go through alternate proposals or sealed bids.

The earlier Receipt Desk post had the eight-camera deployment from local reporting. Auto-Dig later recovered and landed the county's own procurement packet, so now there are receipts for the contract terms too.
