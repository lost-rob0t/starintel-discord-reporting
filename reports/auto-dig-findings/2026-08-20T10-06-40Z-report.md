# RALEIGH POLICE SAY FLOCK HELPED IDENTIFY A MURDER SUSPECT LATER ARRESTED IN PENNSYLVANIA

Raleigh Police say detectives used Flock cameras after an August 13 homicide. The cameras produced investigative leads that helped identify Rickey Andra Harper, who was then charged with first-degree murder.

This is a pending criminal case. Harper has been charged, not convicted.
