# WORLD'S IRIS MATCHING MODEL ASSUMES TWO OF THREE PARTIES DON'T COLLUDE

World's public `iris-mpc` implementation uses three participating parties under a semi-honest, honest-majority security model. The repository says at most one of the three may act adversarially, which means the model assumes multiple parties do not collude.

That is the actual privacy boundary for the published iris matching design. The sensitive matching state is split across parties rather than presented as "nothing biometric-derived exists anywhere."

World's Face Authentication is a separate path. Its help documentation says encrypted face images stay on the user's phone and the face comparison happens locally.

The unresolved part is operational control: the reviewed public sources do not identify every current production legal entity running the three MPC parties or who can replace, reconfigure, or suspend them.
