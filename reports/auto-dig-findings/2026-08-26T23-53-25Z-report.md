# SYRACUSE REMOVED ITS FLOCK READERS. THAT DID NOT MAKE ONONDAGA COUNTY FLOCK-FREE.

Syracuse removed its municipal Flock readers by July. The wider county still has separate ALPR control surfaces.

The Onondaga County Sheriff's Office independently documents license-plate-reader use. The current vendor for that sheriff equipment is not established by the official page reviewed here.

The EFF Atlas of Surveillance lists six Flock readers operated by Onondaga Community College campus safety as of September 2025. Syracuse also had to separately remove a DeWitt-owned Flock reader that sat on city property.

The useful distinction is ownership and control: removing Syracuse's cameras did not prove that every other ALPR network in Onondaga County disappeared with them.
