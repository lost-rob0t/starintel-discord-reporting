# WORLD'S ORB HAS TWO DIFFERENT DATA PATHS

World's current Orb docs say face and eye images are processed on the Orb, encrypted into Personal Custody, sent to the user's phone, then deleted from the Orb.

But that is not the whole data flow. World also says permanently encrypted codes generated from those photos are stored across secure databases to prevent duplicate verification. Separately, its Help Center documents an optional opt-in that sends Orb or authentication photos to Tools for Humanity servers to train and improve World ID models.

So "the Orb deletes the images" and "nothing biometric-derived leaves the device" are different claims. The first is documented. The second is not.
