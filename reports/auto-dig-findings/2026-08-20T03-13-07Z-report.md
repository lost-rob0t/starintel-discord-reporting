# STEWART'S TURNED DOWN FIVE ALPR HOSTING SITES

Rensselaer County Sheriff's Office approached Stewart's Shops in August 2025 about placing license plate readers at five stores in the county. By December, Stewart's had declined.

Stewart's spokesperson Robin Cooper said customer privacy concerns were part of the decision and questioned whether a private company should be hosting this kind of law-enforcement system instead of government property. Times Union and WAMC both report the rejection.

Hudson Police separately approached Stewart's about a Columbia County site. Stewart's declined that request too.

These were proposed private hosting sites, not deployments. The reviewed evidence does not establish Stewart's ownership of the cameras, Flock administration, or plate-query access.
