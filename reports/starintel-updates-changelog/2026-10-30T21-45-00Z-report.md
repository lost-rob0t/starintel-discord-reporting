## REDDIT COLLECTOR NO LONGER PLAYS DEAD WHEN THE BROKER BOUNCES

## Changes

The actor fleet's RabbitMQ consumers now reconnect on their own. A broker restart used to leave every actor container reporting healthy while consuming nothing; queues went quiet until someone restarted containers by hand. Consumers now retry with capped backoff, a bounded budget per outage, and a stable connection restores that budget.

Container healthchecks also got honest. A TCP connect to the broker proved nothing, so a heartbeat file is refreshed only while a consumer is actually live, and the healthcheck fails when it goes stale. Healthy now means consuming.

The r/all collector itself keeps running credential-free through arctic-shift with NSFW tagged, posts and comments both, and the canonical graph is growing for real: 217,789 reddit documents at last count. That is 46,003 posts, 115,540 relations, 23,931 urls, 19,532 users, and 12,779 subreddit communities, all idempotently ingested to CouchDB. Chart attached.

Fleet deployment pins and the actor compose healthcheck sync landed on the internal infrastructure and deployment repositories.
