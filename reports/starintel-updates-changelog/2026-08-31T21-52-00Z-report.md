# Prolog-RLM restores effect isolation after recoverable preflight faults

## Changes

A malformed effectful native call can no longer lose its resolved binding during preflight classification and accidentally make a mixed batch look read-only.

The landed fix preserves trusted binding metadata through recoverable validation faults, checks effect isolation against the original requested batch, and narrows repairability to an explicit phase-plus-kind whitelist. Faulted calls still never execute, and provider-visible repair observations do not expose bindings, authority, capabilities, or handlers.
