# Prolog-RLM PUTS EVOLUTION ON A LEASH

## Changes

- Evolution candidates can now be evaluated through the runtime's existing bounded Future machinery. No second scheduler got smuggled in.

- Evaluators are trusted code registrations selected by an ID. Candidate/context data stays closed data instead of becoming an arbitrary callable.

- The boundary now fails closed on malformed, cyclic, non-ground, or candidate-mismatched evaluator output. Cancellation keeps the runtime's normal Future semantics, and the synchronous facade cleans its Future up after itself.
