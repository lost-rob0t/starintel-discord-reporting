## AUTO-DIG NOW HAS A SUPERVISED PROLOG ACTOR

## Changes

Auto-Dig can now snapshot the `investigation-target` queue, let a supervised Prolog actor choose work, and persist actor state across runs. The actor also applies recurrence rules so normal/low targets do not run twice in a row while high-priority work may repeat.

Model routing is owned by a Prolog expert KB and the selected model plus reasoning effort are passed into the pinned Prolog-RLM runtime. Successful runs push their own branch and record the actor decision, model route, and runtime trace.

This is the orchestration slice, not full autonomous research yet. The merged PR explicitly leaves tool-enabled MCP research and canonical StarIntel writers for the next gated slice.
