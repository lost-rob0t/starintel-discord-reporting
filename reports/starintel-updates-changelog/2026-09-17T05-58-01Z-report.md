## StarIntel Wear: v0.3.0-alpha and native Quasar Android

## Changes
- Quasar is now a separately installable native Android app. Sign in with a username/password or a StarIntel API key; credentials are checked before replacing the saved login.
- The phone companion gains an app catalog with verified downloads and an Android installer flow. Downloads are checked for size, SHA-256, package identity, downgrade, and signer continuity. Installation still requires your approval.
- The alpha release includes the Wear app, phone companion, Quasar, and Command, Neon, and Terminal watch faces. Use the Wear APK on the watch, not the phone.
