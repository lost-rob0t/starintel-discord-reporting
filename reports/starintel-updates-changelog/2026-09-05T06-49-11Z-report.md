## 🎯 StarIntel: the end state

**Type:** `ROADMAP`

- **Symbolic first.** Prolog-RLM and domain experts should handle facts, rules, planning, verification, and known failure paths. LLMs fill the language and open-ended reasoning gaps instead of being the only engine.
- **Development becomes an expert-controlled loop.** Task Steward chooses and sequences work while implementation, RAGE, research, and independent review workers execute specialized roles.
- **Self-hosted development becomes the default.** Source control, CI, deployment, synchronization, and internal services should operate as one reproducible environment.
- **Zara becomes the human interface.** Desktop, Android, voice, plugins, session history, memory, and StarIntel tools converge into one persistent assistant.
- **StarIntel becomes the durable intelligence layer.** Actors, graph data, documents, research, symbolic memory, and expert systems share one extensible runtime instead of living as disconnected experiments.

The target is not a chatbot with more tools. It is a system where knowledge is durable, actions are typed, experts are reusable, agents can work for long periods, and important results can be independently verified.
