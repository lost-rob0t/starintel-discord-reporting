# Hackmode gives HTTP providers a typed transport policy

## Changes

HTTP providers can now declare whether they support proxies, SOCKS, browser/CDP transport, direct-only operation, and supported capture modes through one typed policy instead of ad hoc backend behavior.

Client profiles are versioned and validated for coherent HTTP/capture settings, with deterministic defaults and sensitive headers filtered from persisted provenance.
