## AUTO-RESEARCH FINDS STARINTEL'S IDEMPOTENCY IS STILL SPLIT ACROSS SUBSYSTEMS

## Changes

Research found StarIntel already has useful duplicate-suppression pieces in target creation, lease requests, dispatch fingerprints, durable IDs, and outbox-style effects, but no single application-level idempotency contract spanning HTTP retries, Rabbit redelivery, persistence, scheduled executions, and ambiguous failures.

The design proposes one runtime-owned `IdempotencyRepository` plus a small state machine: one claimant gets execution authority, same-key/same-request duplicates replay or wait, same-key/different-request conflicts fail before mutation, and ambiguous outcomes stay `outcome-unknown` until authoritative reconciliation proves what happened. Timeout or restart does not magically turn an unknown effect back into a fresh operation.

It also keeps fencing separate from idempotency. Replaying an accepted request cannot make a stale lease generation authoritative again.
