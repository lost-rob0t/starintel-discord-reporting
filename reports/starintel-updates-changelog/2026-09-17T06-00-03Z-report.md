# StarLang: Common Lisp and Nim actors meet over ZMQ

## Changes
- A bounded Common Lisp libzmq binding and managed Nim actor sessions have landed.
- External-actor calls lower through real mailboxes, with transport-owned readiness and cross-language JSON lifecycle framing. The change includes end-to-end coverage and standalone Nix outputs.
- Canonical JSON gains the RFC 8785 binary64 foundation.

The number-serialization change is a foundation slice, not a claim that the entire canonicalization or language roadmap is finished.
