# Hackmode: replay checks the bytes it resumes

## Changes
- New capture-replay checkpoints record a SHA-256 fingerprint and the size of the consumed spool prefix.
- Replay verifies that evidence before resuming. A replaced, shortened, or mismatching spool raises a typed error instead of decoding unrelated bytes into canonical evidence.

Legacy checkpoints without a fingerprint retain the weaker size-only check. The stronger guarantee applies to the new fingerprinted checkpoints, not retroactively to evidence that never recorded a hash.
